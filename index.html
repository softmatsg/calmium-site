CALMIUM

Place the slabs. Fire. Stay calm.

Calmium is a small physics puzzle for Android. A round is fired down a lane; you place slabs of water, gel, wood, sand, steel and more, and try to stop it in the right place. Order, gaps and thickness matter in surprising ways.

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Chapters 1–2 are free; one purchase, Calmium Lab, unlocks the rest.
