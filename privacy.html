Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026. Publisher: Thulge Labs.

Calmium is an offline puzzle game. We do not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and it does not use the internet itself.
  • Your progress stays on your device in one file (stars, Calmium, codex, settings). If Android backup is on for your phone, Google may include that file in your device backup so your progress survives a phone change. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we never see it.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. When you buy Calmium Lab, payment details go to Google, not to us. The app only learns whether the purchase succeeded.
  • Children: the app is rated for ages 13+ and collects no data from anyone.

Questions: contact Thulge Labs through the developer email shown on the Calmium page in Google Play.
